- kernel family: positional encoding (RoPE, GPT-J/NEOX variants with NOPE support)
- concise summary: Tiles the sequence in Triton, loads per-position frequencies, and applies rotary mixing on each head channel while optionally preserving non-rotary features at the front or back of the vector.
- detailed summary: `_rope_fwd` inspects the frequency tensor to infer whether non-rotary (NOPE) channels are present, chooses BLOCK_S=32 and a head-aligned BLOCK_D/2 size, and launches `_rope_kernel_sbhd_fwd` over (batch × head × seq tiles). Each program loads the relevant cos/sin values—reusing the front half if requested—fetches the corresponding slice of `x`, constructs the rotated companion either in GPT-J ordering (pairwise even/odd) or NEOX ordering (swizzled halves), computes `x*cos + x_rotated*sin`, and writes back to `out` while copying untouched NOPE channels if operating out-of-place.
