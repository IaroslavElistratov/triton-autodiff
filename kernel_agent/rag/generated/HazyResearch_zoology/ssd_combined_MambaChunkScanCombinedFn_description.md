- kernel family: selective scan pipeline (Mamba combined forward)
- concise summary: Orchestrates the full forward pass of the Mamba-style selective scan by preprocessing dt, building chunk states, propagating them between chunks, forming chunk-wise transition matrices, and executing the gated scan, optionally returning final or variable-length states.
- detailed summary: The driver first calls _chunk_cumsum_fwd to softplus-constrain and clamp the dt stream while generating per-chunk dA_cumsum; it then invokes _chunk_state_fwd to contract inputs with B, and _state_passing_fwd to propagate those states across chunks (with optional initial states and ragged sequence handling). With the propagated states reshaped back to (batch, nchunks, heads, headdim, dstate), it forms chunk transition matrices via _bmm_chunk_fwd(C, B, chunk_size) and feeds them, along with X, dt, dA_cumsum, chunk states, optional skip weights D, and gate tensor Z into _chunk_scan_fwd, which yields the selective scan output and, when gating is enabled, the ungated activations. If requested, the function also returns final chunk states or gathers per-sequence variable-length states. The wrapper manages dtype casting, layout reshaping, and options such as dt_bias, dt_softplus, seq_idx, cu_seqlens, and return_final_states.
