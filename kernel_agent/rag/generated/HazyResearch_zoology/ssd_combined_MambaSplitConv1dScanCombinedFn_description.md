- kernel family: selective scan pipeline with convolutional front-end
- concise summary: Splits a packed tensor containing non-SSM, gating, and state-space parameters, runs a causal 1-D convolution to produce the B/C/x streams, executes the combined Mamba scan, and optionally applies RMS normalization, gating, and an output projection.
- detailed summary: The forward pass unpacks zxbcdt into SWIGLU inputs, gating activations, concatenated (B‖C) features, and dt logits, applies causal_conv1d_cuda.causal_conv1d_fwd to obtain per-step x/B/C slices, reshapes them into (batch, seqlen, nheads, headdim) and (batch, seqlen, ngroups, dstate), and runs _mamba_chunk_scan_combined_fwd with dt_softplus=True, optional initial states, skip weights D, and gating tensor z. When rmsnorm_weight is provided, the ungated SSM activations out_x are flattened, RMS-normalized together with z (respecting the norm_before_gate flag), optionally concatenated with the non-SSM SWIGLU branch, and reshaped back; otherwise out_x is immediately gated (if z exists) and concatenated. The routine finally applies an optional linear projection (honouring autocast), returns the scan output (and optionally final states), and records the intermediates needed later in the training step.
