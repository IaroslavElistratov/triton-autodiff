- kernel family: normalization (LayerNorm with affine parameters, row-parallel Triton kernel)
- concise summary: Reshapes the tensor into rows, launches a single-row kernel that computes mean/variance in FP32, normalizes, and applies weight and bias while caching the inverse std and mean per row.
- detailed summary: `calculate_settings` selects `BLOCK_SIZE = next_power_of_2(dim)` (capped by `MAX_FUSED_SIZE`) and an appropriate warp count; the forward launcher allocates output plus FP32 buffers for `inv_var` (`r`) and the mean (`mu`) before invoking `layernorm_forward`. Each program (`program_id(0)=row`) loads the row, weight, and bias tiles under a mask, sums to get `mean_X`, centers the row to form `XX`, computes the variance/rsqrt with epsilon, stores the inverse std and mean to the buffers, and writes `(XX * inv_var) * W + b` back to `Y`. The reshape/restore steps keep the original tensor shape while ensuring row-major access for the kernel.
