- kernel family: layer normalization (fused mean/variance + scale)
- concise summary: The forward kernel normalizes each feature row in-place by computing mean and variance in fp32, applying an epsilon-stabilized reciprocal stddev, and multiplying by the learned weight vector.
- detailed summary: `_layer_norm_fwd_fused` maps each `tl.program_id(0)` to a row of the reshaped `[M, N]` activation, loads the row into registers in `BLOCK_SIZE` slices, and accumulates both the sum and squared deviation in float32 to obtain the mean and variance; the fma-based numerator `x - mean` and denominator `sqrt(var + 1e-5)` yield `xhat`, which is then multiplied by the corresponding weight tile. Masked loads handle feature counts smaller than the power-of-two tile, and the kernel also writes out the per-row mean and rstd into side buffers for backward reuse. The launcher flattens batch dimensions, allocates those statistics, picks an autotuned `BLOCK_SIZE` (up to 64 KB worth of elements), and dispatches the kernel with `num_warps` chosen to match the tile width.
